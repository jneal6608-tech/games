# Short-form video playbook (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

The game was built to be filmed. This is how to use that.

## Why it works on camera

| Property | In Catangle |
|---|---|
| Understood in 2 seconds, sound off | Cats with faces on a grid; one tap sends one away |
| A question the viewer answers in their head | "Which cat can move?" Viewers comment to correct mistakes |
| Satisfying payoff | Rapid clears raise each meow's pitch and show a combo counter, then confetti |
| Funny failure | A blocked cat goes cross-eyed with a "Bonk!" |
| Recognisable | Tangled long cats are the brand; no logo needed |
| Vertical by design | Portrait board; clip mode keeps the lower third clear for platform buttons |

## How to make a clip

1. In the game: Settings, then Clip mode. It auto-plays your current level with a clean screen and an end card.
2. Record with your phone's screen recorder, sound on. This is the best quality.
3. Or from a computer: `tools/make_clips.mjs` records clips with a caption. Edit the list at the top of the file. These are silent and softer; add audio in your editor.
4. Address-bar form for custom clips: `?clip=45&caption=Your+hook&fails=1` (`fails=1` adds one deliberate bonk).

Three sample clips are in `marketing/clips/`. The end card says "Free on Google Play", so post them only once the game is live, or trim the end.

## Formats to rotate

| Format | Hook text | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge | "Which cat moves first? 99% bonk it" | Pause on the full board for a second before the first tap |
| Fail bait | "I can't get past level 30" | Play badly on purpose; viewers install to prove they can |
| Satisfying | "POV: you finally untangle the cats" | Big Level, fast clear, trending calm audio |
| Countdown | "Free the grumpy one before it hits 0" | Grumpy cat levels (30, 34, 38...) |
| Cat people | "Every cat owner at 3am" | Sleepy cats refusing to move; real cat footage stitched in |
| Collection | "Rating every cat in my game" | Scroll the collection; ask which coat to add next |
| Dev diary | "I made a game about long cats" | Face or voice-over; these build followers, the others get installs |

## Rules of thumb

- First frame is the full board, never a logo or menu.
- Hook text on screen in the first second, six words or fewer.
- 8 to 15 seconds. End just after the confetti.
- Post 3 to 5 times a week for at least six weeks before judging it.
- Reply to comments with a video. "Make a level where..." is free content.
- Link in bio straight to the Play listing. Use the same name and icon everywhere.
- Free reach elsewhere: cat communities on Reddit and Facebook (read each group's rules on self-promotion), and puzzle-game Discords.

No clip is guaranteed to perform. The aim is many cheap attempts.
