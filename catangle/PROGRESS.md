## What exists

- Endless generated levels, three hearts, sleepy and grumpy cats, shaped boards, Big Levels.
- Daily Tangle with streaks, 16 coats, 4 rooms, hints and treats, fish economy.
- Tutorial for the first three levels and intro cards for new cat types.
- Save and load with backup, sound, vibration, settings, reset.
- Clip mode for recording short-form video, and three sample clips in `marketing/clips/`.
- Launcher icons, 512 px store icon, feature graphic, store listing text, privacy policy draft.
