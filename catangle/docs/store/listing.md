**App name (30 max):** Catangle: Cat Escape Puzzle

**Short description (80 max):** Untangle the cats! Tap the right cat, clear the board, collect them all.

**Full description:**

A pile of very long, very tangled cats is blocking the room. Can you send them all home?

Tap a cat and it runs straight ahead. If the way is clear, it zooms off the board. If another cat is in the way: bonk! Three bonks and the cats get cranky.

HOW TO PLAY
• Look where each cat is facing
• Tap a cat with a clear path
• Clear the whole board without losing your three hearts

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Easy to learn in seconds, tricky to master
• Sleepy cats that only wake when others leave
• Grumpy cats on a countdown: free them first!
• A new Daily Tangle every day, with streak rewards
• 16 cat coats and 4 cosy rooms to collect
• Endless levels, playable offline
• No timers, no lives to wait for

Relaxing when you want it, a real brain teaser when you don't. If you like arrow puzzles, escape puzzles, tap-away games or just cats, Catangle is for you.

Catangle is free to play with optional ads and purchases.
