## Full description (4,000 characters max)
Build the sky. Light one fuse.

Fuseworks is a puzzle roguelike about staging the perfect firework display. Place shells on the night sky, pick where the fuse starts, and watch every burst set off the next in one long, glorious chain reaction.

NO FORCED ADS. EVER.
Fuseworks never interrupts you. The only ads are ones you choose to watch for a bonus, and one small purchase removes even those. Plays fully offline.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Drag firework shells from your hand onto the sky. Each shell shows exactly which cells its burst will reach.
• Tap a shell to preview the chain, then light the fuse.
• Sparkle × Awe = applause. Longer chains mean more Awe. Matching colours ring out in Harmony.
• Your display stays in the sky. Every Match you add more shells and the whole thing fires again, bigger and louder.
• Hit the applause target before you run out of Matches.

BUILD A SHOW NOBODY HAS SEEN BEFORE
• 28 charms that bend the rules: double the final Match, reward single-colour chains, make the first shell burst twice, turn spare coins into Awe.
• 14 kinds of shell, from the humble Peony to the row-and-column-clearing Dragon.
• Refine your favourites, trim the weak ones, and shape a crate that breaks the scoreboard.
• Star cells, low clouds, crosswinds and tough crowds change every sky.

ALWAYS ONE MORE RUN
• A six-night festival that is different every time.
• Endless mode: no final night. How far can your display carry you?
• Five stakes of rising difficulty.
• A Daily Show: one sky, the same for everyone, with medals and a streak to protect.
• Unlock new shells, charms, starting crates and skies as you play.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• One thumb, portrait, quick sessions.
• Every firework is drawn and every sound is synthesised live, so the game is a tiny download.
• Colours are paired with shapes, so the game does not rely on colour alone.
• Reduced-effects mode for older phones and long battery life.

Fuseworks Premium is a one-time purchase: no ads at all, a free Encore every run, doubled tickets, a free Market reroll every visit and the Aurora sky.

Light it up.
