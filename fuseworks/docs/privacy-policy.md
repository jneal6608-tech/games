# Fuseworks Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Fuseworks ("the game") is published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

## Summary
- The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
- We do not run any servers for the game and we do not receive your gameplay data.
- Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
- The game shows advertising only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which processes some device data when an ad is requested.
- An optional one-time purchase is processed by Google Play.

## Information stored on your device
Game progress, unlocks, records and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. If device backup is enabled in your Android settings, Android may include this data in your own Google backup. We cannot access it. You can delete it at any time with "Erase progress" in the game's settings, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the game.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process: your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and may personalise ads depending on your choices and region.
- Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads).
- Players who own Premium are never shown ads, and once Premium is active on a device the game stops requesting them.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)
If you buy Fuseworks Premium, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive confirmation that the purchase exists so the game can unlock it. We never see your payment details.

## Children
The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

## Sharing
"Share result" opens your device's own share sheet with a short text you can choose to send. The game sends nothing by itself.

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
