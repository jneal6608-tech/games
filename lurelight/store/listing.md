Copy these into Play Console > Grow > Store presence > Main store listing. Character limits are Google's.

## App name (30 max)

Lurelight: Deep Sea Survivor

## Short description (80 max)

Whirl your glowing lure, mutate, and dive as deep as you dare. One thumb.

## Full description (4000 max)

You are a small anglerfish in a very big, very hungry ocean. Your glowing lure is your only weapon.

SWING THE LIGHT
Drag anywhere to swim. Circle your thumb and the lure whirls around you; the faster you whirl, the harder it hits. No auto-fire, no buttons: one thumb, and every hit is yours.

MUTATE
Eat plankton, level up, and choose a mutation: a heavier lure, a second lure, shockwaves, poison trails, chain lightning, a whip-crack that cuts through a whole swarm. Every mutation changes how your lure looks, so no two dives look the same.

DIVE DEEPER
Twilight Zone. Midnight Zone. The Abyss. The Trench. Each depth has new creatures to learn: darters that telegraph a dash, squid that take aim, armoured urchins that only a fast swing can crack. A boss guards every zone, and past the Trench the dive never ends.

MAKE IT YOURS
- Unlock new fish that handle differently: a heavyweight that ploughs through swarms, a fragile whip that snaps to your thumb, a twin with two lures from the first second
- Spend pearls on permanent upgrades, small enough that skill still decides
- 25 awards to chase
- A new Daily Dive every day: the same ocean for everyone
- Daily rewards and streaks

FAIR BY DESIGN
- No energy timers. Play as much as you like
- No loot boxes, no gear gacha
- Ads are optional: watch one to revive, double your pearls or reroll a choice, or never watch one at all
- A short ad may appear between dives, never during one. A one-time purchase removes them for good
- Plays offline

Built for short sessions: a dive takes two to five minutes. Clip Mode hides the interface for clean screen recordings.

How deep can you go?
