Lurelight ("the game") is published by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles. Questions: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your progress (best depth, pearls, unlocks, settings) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the AdMob software inside the game may collect and send to Google:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city level) can be inferred
  • how you interact with ads and with the game (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped)
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data

Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, personalise ads. How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change that choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad Privacy Choices.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (the exact place depends on your Android version).
  • The rewarded ads are always optional. The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase removes the ads shown between dives.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google is governed by Google's policies above; uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.
