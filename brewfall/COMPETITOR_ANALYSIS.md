# Competitor analysis

Based on public coverage found on 11–12 Nov 2026. I did not play these games or see their private numbers; pricing and status should be re-checked on the stores before launch.

| Game | Platform / price (as reported) | What it is | What it proves | Where Brewfall differs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Balatro** | Mobile, premium (~$10), no ads/IAP | Poker-hand roguelike score-builder, solo-developed | The format retains and spreads by word of mouth; public mobile revenue figures run from $4.4M (2 months) to $20M+ | Free to play; one-thumb physics instead of card selection; shorter sessions |
| **Dogpile** | PC, then iOS/Android | Suika-style dog merging + deckbuilding with ~100 tags | The exact hybrid has an audience; closest direct competitor | Brewfall keeps the cauldron between brews, has score targets with rival hexes, and is built free-to-play with a meta economy |
| **Fruitbearer** | PC (launched autumn 2026) | Darker Suika roguelike deckbuilder, 50+ relics | More entrants = validated demand | PC-first; not F2P mobile |
| **SIGMA COALESCE, Catomi** | Announced for 2026 | Suika + Slay-the-Spire style | The niche is heating up; being early on Google Play matters | Brewfall can ship now |
| **CloverPit** | Mobile $4.99 | Slot-machine roguelike; 1M+ on Steam | Appetite for "numbers go up" builders | No gambling theme, so no simulated-gambling rating issue |
| **Dice of Kalma** | Mobile/Steam, 1M+ downloads | Dice Balatro-like | A small team can reach 1M downloads in this genre | Different core toy |
| **Suika-style clones** (Evo Merge X, Orb Smash, many more) | Free, ad-funded | Plain drop-merge | Huge casual demand for the toy | They have no build depth or progression; Brewfall adds a reason to return |
| **Merge-2 leaders** (Travel Town, Gossip Harbor) | Free, IAP-heavy | Board merge with story | Merge is the fastest-growing big subgenre | Different game; we borrow the "merge" search demand, not the model |

## Takeaways used in the design

1. **Readable scoring.** Every merge shows Potency x Mult; charm order matters (left to right), copied from what makes Balatro's builds legible.
2. **Short brews, long runs.** A brew is 30–90 seconds; a run is 24 brews with auto-save, which suits mobile sessions.
3. **Free with fair monetisation** is the differentiator against premium incumbents; aggressive ads would throw that away.
4. **Store keywords** should lead with "merge" (demand) and "roguelike" (intent), see `store/listing.md`.

## Gaps I could not verify
