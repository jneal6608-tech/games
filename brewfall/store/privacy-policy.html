Summary

Brewfall has no accounts and we do not run any servers that receive your data. Your game progress is stored on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play; those Google services process some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, currency balances and a list of delivered purchase tokens are saved locally. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Google may include this save file in your device backup. You can erase it at any time in Settings > Erase all progress, or by uninstalling the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address (which indicates approximate location), app activity such as ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Depending on your region and your choices, this may be used for personalised advertising. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent before requesting ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation (product, order ID and purchase token) which is checked and stored on your device so that items are delivered once.

Children

Brewfall is not directed at children under 13 and should be listed for ages 13+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data on our own systems, requests about ad data should be directed to Google using the controls above. For any question contact [CONTACT EMAIL].
