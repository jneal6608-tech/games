# Emberhold privacy policy (draft; have it reviewed before publishing)

Last updated: [DATE]

Emberhold is published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we"). Contact: [EMAIL].

**What the game stores.** Your progress (Embers, gems, Workshop upgrades, unlocked flames, quests and settings) is saved on your device. We do not run servers and do not receive it. If device backup is switched on, Google may back it up to your Google account.

**Advertising.** The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising id, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the Settings screen (privacy options). You can also reset or delete your advertising id in your Android settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Purchases.** Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation of a purchase, not your payment details.

**Children.** Emberhold is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

**Your rights.** Depending on where you live you may have rights to access or delete data held about you. Because we hold none ourselves, requests about advertising data should go to Google; contact us if you need help.

**Changes.** We will update this page if the policy changes.
