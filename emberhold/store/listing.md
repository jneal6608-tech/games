# Google Play listing

**Title (30):** Emberhold: Idle Defense

**Short description (80):** Keep the last fire burning. Idle roguelite defense with no forced ads.

**Full description:**

The night is full of shades, and yours is the last fire burning.

Emberhold is an idle defense game with a roguelite heart. Your Ember fights on its own. You decide how it grows.

HOLD THE LIGHT
• Shades pour in from the dark. Everything inside your light gets burned.
• Spend Cinders mid-run on damage, speed, health, crits, light radius and more.
• Every 5 waves, choose one of three boons: chain sparks, burning hits, a pulse of fire, a second wind.

EVERY RUN MAKES YOU STRONGER
• Embers are permanent. Upgrade the Workshop and start each run further ahead.
• Unlock five flames that play differently, from the chaining Stormflame to the fragile, deadly Voidflame.
• Master three abilities: Flare, Ward and Stoke.
• Six difficulties, each darker and richer than the last.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Idle friendly: your keepers gather Embers while you are away.
• Speed up to 3×, auto-pick boons, or make every choice yourself.
• Daily quests, a seven-day gift calendar and dozens of achievements.

FAIR BY DESIGN
• No forced ads. Ever. Videos are optional and always give something back.
• Everything can be earned by playing.
• Plays offline.

ACCESSIBLE
• Text size, high contrast, reduced motion, left-handed layout, and enemies you can tell apart by shape.
